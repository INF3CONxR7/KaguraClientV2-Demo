How to use Kaura4.0 (Demonstration Only on Demo and can't be used for attack players!)

1. specifying the player
In case of button type
To select a player, press "L1 & cross down".
If you specify a player, you can attack that player with SingleAura.
You can also add a player to the TargetList.
Viewpoint type
Press and hold "L2 & Cross Left" to select a player from the viewpoint.
While pressing "L2 & Cross Left", the player will be zoomed in a little.
When you look at the player, it will be targeted. (Partially finish)

2. about TargetList
TargetList can register up to 100 players.
Registered players can be attacked by TargetAura.
To register a player, press "L2 & cross down".
To unregister a player, press "L2 & cross down" again.

About SingleAura
SingleAura can attack a specific player.
To attack, press "R2 & Cross Up" once.
To attack automatically, press "Cross Up x 5 briefly" and it will attack every certain period of time.
To cancel the automatic attack, "Cross UP x 5 briefly" again.

About InfiniteAura
InfiniteAura attacks all players in the world.
To attack, press "R2 & Cross Left" once.
To attack automatically, press "Cross Left x 5 briefly" and it will attack every certain period of time.
To cancel the automatic attack, press "Cross Left x 5 briefly" again.

About TargetAura
TargetAura attacks all players registered in the TargetList.
To attack, press "R2 & cross down" once.
To attack automatically, press "Cross down x 5 briefly" and it will attack every certain period of time.
To cancel the automatic attack, press "Cross down x 5 briefly" again.

6. about AttackSpeed, MultiAttack, and HitRate
AttackSpeed can be adjusted in 10 steps.
Press "Cross Left & R1" to increase the speed.
"Cross Left & L1" to decrease the speed.

MultiAttack" can attack multiple times when attacking once.
The number of attacks can be adjusted in 10 steps.
Cross Up & R1" to increase the number of attacks.
Cross Up & L1" to decrease the number of attacks.

HitRate allows you to multiplicate attack.
You can adjust the number of attacks to 5 levels, but if you increase it too much, the server will be overloaded.
"Cross Right & R1" increases the accuracy (which is very expensive).
"Cross Right & L1" reduces the accuracy (reduces the load).

All this functionnalities is available on the Premium Version of KaguraClient V2.
